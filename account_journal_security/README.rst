This module restricts each accounting journal to a list of user groups,
so that each person of the finance team only sees and posts in some
journals: the billing team in the sales journals, the treasury team in
the bank journals, and so on.

A journal with **Allowed Groups** is visible only to the users of one of
those groups. The restriction also covers what is posted in the journal:
journal entries (invoices and bills included), journal items, payments,
bank statement lines and the invoices analysis. A journal without
allowed groups stays visible to every user, so installing the module
changes nothing until a journal is configured.

The restriction is a global record rule, added on top of the
multi-company rules of Odoo, which stay as they are. It applies to every
user, the accounting administrators included: an exception is made by
data, adding the group of the administrators to the journal. Only the
superuser (code running with sudo) ignores it.

Configuration
=============

1. Create one group per team in *Settings > Users & Companies > Groups*
   (for instance *Billing team* and *Treasury team*) and add the users.
2. In *Invoicing > Configuration > Journals*, open a journal, go to the
   **Advanced Settings** tab and fill **Allowed Groups** in the **Access
   by Group** section. The field is visible to accounting
   administrators.

Before restricting a journal, add to it a group that the administrators
belong to: an administrator outside the allowed groups no longer sees
the journal, including in the configuration menu.

Usage
=====

A user sees a journal, and the entries, journal items, payments and bank
statement lines posted in it, when the journal has no allowed groups or
when the user belongs to one of its allowed groups. The other journals
do not appear in lists, searches, selections or reports, and opening or
creating a record in them raises an access error.

The default journal of a new invoice, bill or payment is chosen among
the journals visible to the user. When a new invoice or bill would use a
journal type for which the user has no visible journal, the error
message says that none of the groups of the user is allowed in a journal
of that type.

Changes of the allowed groups of a journal, or of the groups of a user,
apply immediately, without restarting the server.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- Accounting reports (trial balance, general ledger, MIS Builder and
  others) read journal items with the rights of the user: for a
  restricted user they only show the visible journals and come out
  partial.
- Every user who posts needs at least one visible journal of each type
  he uses: an invoice needs a sales journal, a bill a purchase journal.
- Registering a payment needs a visible bank or cash journal.
- Reconciliation between journals of different groups (for instance an
  invoice and a payment of another team) and the Enterprise accounting
  screens were not evaluated.
- Bank statements (the statement records, not their lines), analytic
  items and reconciliation models are not restricted.
- For 19.0, the groups of a user are in another field (``group_ids``
  instead of ``groups_id``): the record rules have to be adapted in the
  migration.
- To choose the default journal among the visible ones, the journal of
  entries and payments is computed with the rights of the user instead
  of as superuser. A module that overrides that computation and reads
  records the user cannot access has to be checked.
