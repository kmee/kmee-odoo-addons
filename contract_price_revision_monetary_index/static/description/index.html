Contract Price Revision by Monetary Index

This module revises the prices of recurring contracts on their anniversary by a monetary index (a consumer price index such as IPCA, INPC or IGP-M, or any index of Monetary Index Update), without typing the percentage.

The revision uses the mechanism of Contract Price Revision: the current line ends on the day before the revision date and a successor line starts with the revised price, so the price history stays in the contract and invoices already issued do not change.

The variation is the compound variation of the monthly rates of the index over the months of the period (by default the twelve months that end in the month before the revision). While any month of the period is not published, the revision waits.

Each contract chooses how the revision is applied: an activity for the responsible to review and approve (default), or automatically on the revision date. It works for customer and supplier contracts.

Configuration

The rates of the index must be in Monetary Index Update: typed by hand or fetched by a provider module (for Brazil, monetary_index_update_bacen fetches IPCA, INPC, IGP-M and SELIC from the Banco Central do Brasil).

On the contract, open the Price Revision tab and set:

  • Revision Index: the index used to revise the prices.
  • Revision Interval (months): months between two revisions, 12 by default. It is also the number of monthly rates accumulated.
  • Index Lag (months): months between the end of the accumulated period and the month of the revision. With 1, a revision in December uses the rates up to November; with 2, up to October. Consumer price indexes are usually published around the 10th of the following month, so a lag of 2 lets the revision be prepared before its date.
  • Revision Base Date: date of the current price (the contract start by default). The Next Revision is the base date plus the interval.
  • Negative Variation: do not reduce prices (default) or apply the negative variation.
  • Revision Cap (%): maximum variation of one revision; zero means no cap.
  • Revision Mode: schedule an activity for approval (default) or apply automatically.

Usage

A daily scheduled action looks for contracts whose next revision is within five days and whose index has every rate of the period published.

Approval mode (default). The action schedules one Contract revision by index activity for the contract responsible (or, without a responsible, for the user who created the contract), due on the revision date, with the index, period, accumulated factor and percentage.

To approve, click Revise by Index on the contract. The wizard of Contract Price Revision opens with the Monetary Index variation type and a preview of each selected contract. Click Apply.

The current line ends on the day before the revision date and a new line starts with the revised price. The new line keeps the index, the period, the factor and the percentage applied; the calculation is also posted in the contract chatter, the base date moves to the revision date and the activity is marked as done.

Automatic mode. On the revision date the scheduled action applies the revision without the wizard, with the same result.

The revision can also be run by hand at any time, for one contract with Revise by Index or for several contracts with the action Create revision of contract lines and the Monetary Index variation type.

Known issues / Roadmap

  • An invoice generated before the revision is approved keeps the old price; the difference of that period has to be settled by hand.
  • The accumulated period expects one rate per month (monthly indexes).
  • The revision settings could also live on contract templates, so new contracts start with them.
