To use this module, you need to:

1. Open Accounting > Accounting > Journal Items (or a journal entry).
2. Edit the Due Date of a receivable/payable line. Posted entries require the
   group "Edit due date of posted journal items".
3. After saving a posted entry, check its chatter for the logged change.

The change is refused when the item is reconciled, is not receivable/payable,
or is part of a payment order that is not cancelled.
