## Configuration Guide

To configure the Customer Satisfaction module, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to **Contacts > Configuration > Satisfaction Levels**.
2. Click **Create** to add a new satisfaction level.
3. Fill in the following fields:
   - **Customer Rating Description**: A label to identify the satisfaction level.
   - **Rating Icon**: An image that visually represents the satisfaction level across
     views.
4. Click **Save**.

Once configured, the selected satisfaction level and its corresponding icon will
automatically appear in the contact's form, list, and kanban views, based on the
selected value.
