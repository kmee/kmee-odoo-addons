Registers the health and dental plans of each employee: operator, start
and end dates, amount paid and a description. It works without contracts
or payroll.

The plans are listed in the **Health Plans** tab of the employee,
visible only to HR users, and in *Employees > Employees > Health and
Dental Plans*, where they can be filtered by type and grouped by
operator.

Usage
=====

1. Open an employee and go to the **Health Plans** tab.
2. Add a line for each plan, choosing whether it is health or dental,
   and fill in the operator, the start date and the amount paid.
3. When the employee changes plan, fill in the end date of the old line
   and add a new one.

To see all the plans, go to *Employees > Employees > Health and Dental
Plans*.

With the demo data, open *Marcelo Vieira Cardoso* to see a health plan
and a dental plan.
