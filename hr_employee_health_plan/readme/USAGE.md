1. Open an employee and go to the **Health Plans** tab.
2. Add a line for each plan, choosing whether it is health or dental, and
   fill in the operator, the start date and the amount paid.
3. When the employee changes plan, fill in the end date of the old line and
   add a new one.

To see all the plans, go to *Employees > Employees > Health and Dental Plans*.

With the demo data, open *Marcelo Vieira Cardoso* to see a health plan and a
dental plan.
