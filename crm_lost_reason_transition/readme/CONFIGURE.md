To configure this module, you need to:

1. Go to **CRM > Configuration > Lost Lead Transitions**
2. Click **Create** to add a new transition rule
3. Fill in the following fields:
   - **Loss Reason**: The reason selected when the lead is marked as lost
   - **Source Stage**: The current stage of the lead
   - **Target Team**: The sales team that will handle the recovery
   - **Target Stage**: The stage where the lead should be placed in the recovery team
4. Save the rule

Once configured, the system will automatically redirect lost leads to the defined team
and stage, based on the selected loss reason and current pipeline stage.
