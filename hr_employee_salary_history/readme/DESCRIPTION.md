Keeps the current salary of each employee and the history of its adjustments,
without requiring contracts or payroll.

- The current salary is shown in the **Salary** tab of the employee, visible
  only to HR users.
- The **Adjust Salary** button opens a wizard asking for the date, the reason
  and the new salary. It records the adjustment, with the previous salary and
  the percentage of the increase, and updates the current salary.
- *Employees > Employees > Salary History* lists the adjustments of all
  employees.
