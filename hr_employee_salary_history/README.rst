Keeps the current salary of each employee and the history of its
adjustments, without requiring contracts or payroll.

- The current salary is shown in the **Salary** tab of the employee,
  visible only to HR users.
- The **Adjust Salary** button opens a wizard asking for the date, the
  reason and the new salary. It records the adjustment, with the
  previous salary and the percentage of the increase, and updates the
  current salary.
- *Employees > Employees > Salary History* lists the adjustments of all
  employees.

Usage
=====

To adjust the salary of an employee:

1. Open the employee and go to the **Salary** tab.
2. Click **Adjust Salary**.
3. Fill in the date, the reason (for example, collective bargaining
   adjustment or promotion) and the new salary, and confirm.

The adjustment appears in the history of the tab and the current salary
is updated. To follow all the adjustments, go to *Employees > Employees
> Salary History*.

With the demo data, open *Thiago Augusto Ribeiro* to see three recorded
salaries.
