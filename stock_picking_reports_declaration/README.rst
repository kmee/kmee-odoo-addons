This module allows you to configure a custom declaration text for the
Picking Operations and Delivery Slip reports.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Configuration
=============

Go to "Settings-> General Settings" and scroll to the "Companies"
section.

Clique em "Configure Document Layout", e preencha o campo "Declaration
of Receipt".

Usage
=====

When the Picking Operations and Delivery Slip reports are generated,
they will contain the text of the statement.
