To use this module, you need to:

1. Assign the group "Manage Payment Terms from Purchase" to the users (or to a
   role) that must maintain payment terms. It implies Purchase / User.
2. Those users now see Purchase > Configuration > Payment Terms, where they can
   create and edit payment terms and their lines.

Note: users with the group also see Purchase > Configuration, since the group
is added to that menu, and the submenus that have no groups of their own
(vendor pricelists, product categories). Their other entries keep their own
groups and access rights.
