Simple log of the vacations taken by each employee, for companies that
do not run payroll in Odoo and only need to know who has vacation to
take.

- **Accrual periods** per employee, with the entitled days, the days
  already taken and the remaining balance.
- **Vacations taken** (start, end, days), flagged when part of them is
  sold (cash bonus) or when the first installment of the 13th salary is
  paid with them.
- **Vacation schedule report** by department or employee, to plan the
  next vacations.

It does not depend on the leave or payroll modules. Companies that run
the Brazilian payroll should use ``l10n_br_hr_vacation`` instead.

Usage
=====

To register the accrual periods and the vacations:

1. Open an employee and go to the **Vacations** tab.
2. Add an accrual period with its start date. The end date and the limit
   to start the vacation are filled in automatically and can be changed.
3. Add each vacation taken, choosing the accrual period, the dates, and
   whether it has a cash bonus or the 13th salary advance.

To follow every employee, use *Employees > Employees > Vacation Periods*
(periods with remaining days come first) and *Vacations Taken*. In
*Vacation Periods*, the filters *Expiring Soon* (limit to start the
vacation within 90 days) and *Overdue* (limit already passed) show which
vacations must be scheduled.

To print the schedule:

1. Go to *Employees > Reporting > Vacation Schedule*.
2. Optionally choose departments or employees.
3. Click **Print**. The report lists the periods with remaining days,
   with blank fields to write down the planned start and the options.

With the demo data, check *Fernanda Lima Rocha*, who has a period fully
taken in two installments and another one still open.
