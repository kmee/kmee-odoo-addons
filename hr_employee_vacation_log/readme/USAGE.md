To register the accrual periods and the vacations:

1. Open an employee and go to the **Vacations** tab.
2. Add an accrual period with its start date. The end date and the limit to
   start the vacation are filled in automatically and can be changed.
3. Add each vacation taken, choosing the accrual period, the dates, and
   whether it has a cash bonus or the 13th salary advance.

To follow every employee, use *Employees > Employees > Vacation Periods* (periods with remaining days
come first) and *Vacations Taken*. In *Vacation Periods*, the filters *Expiring Soon* (limit to start
the vacation within 90 days) and *Overdue* (limit already passed) show which vacations must be scheduled.

To print the schedule:

1. Go to *Employees > Reporting > Vacation Schedule*.
2. Optionally choose departments or employees.
3. Click **Print**. The report lists the periods with remaining days, with
   blank fields to write down the planned start and the options.
